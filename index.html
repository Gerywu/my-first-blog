Macroeconomics Research and data analysis

Canada's 1990-91 and 2008 Recessions

A comparative macroeconomic analysis of two Canadian recessions using IS-LM-BP, the Solow model, labour-market evidence, interest rates, exchange rates, and data from Statistics Canada and the Bank of Canada.

R Canadian macro data

Economic Data Analysis in R

A reproducible workflow for cleaning, transforming, and visualizing Canadian GDP, interest-rate, and exchange-rate series. The project includes quarterly growth calculations, recession-focused charts, and clear interpretation of each result.
